AR Video on A4

Use a white A4 sheet with a black X in each corner. Keep the Xs about 2–3 cm wide, and keep them clear of the border line (leave about 1.5 cm of white between each X and the border). Show the sheet to the camera (all four Xs visible), then pick 1, 2, 3 or 5 at the bottom.
